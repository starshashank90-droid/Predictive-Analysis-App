# crazeit Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 26 November 2026

crazeit ("the app") is published by **Crazeit** ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and what happens to it.

## Summary

* Your data stays on your computer.
* We do not collect, receive or sell your personal information.
* The app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

## Data you enter into the app

The app lets you paste or upload tables of data (for example CSV files) and run statistical analyses on them. All calculations happen on your own device. The data you enter and the results are **not sent to us or to any third party**.

When the app starts it asks you to type a name. This name is only shown inside the app window. It is kept in the app's memory while the app is open and is **not saved or sent anywhere**.

If you choose to download a PDF report, it is saved to the location you choose on your device. We do not receive a copy.

## Information stored on your device

The app writes a small technical log file (for example start-up messages and error details) to its own local application data folder on your device. This file is **not sent to us**. It stays on your device, and you may attach it to a support request if you choose to.

## Internet connections

The app does not send your data over the internet. It runs a local engine that only accepts connections from your own computer (address 127.0.0.1), and nothing is exposed to your network.

The app's optional "Explain result" feature produces a short explanation of a result. It works on your device using a built-in summary. If you have installed the free Ollama program on your computer, the app may use it, also on your device. Ollama and any AI model you download are provided by third parties and are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

## Microsoft Store

If you got the app from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may collect information about your download and use of the app, as described in the Microsoft Privacy Statement. We do not receive your personal information from Microsoft.

## Third-party components

The app includes open-source software libraries that run locally on your device. They do not send your data anywhere through the app.

## Children

The app is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

## Your choices

Because we do not collect your personal information, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can remove the app and its log file at any time by uninstalling the app from Windows settings and deleting the folder named crazeit in your local application data folder.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed. If the app changes in a way that affects your data, we will update this policy first.
